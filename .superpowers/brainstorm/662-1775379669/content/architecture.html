Site Architecture — Full Immersive 3D

One persistent Three.js canvas. The camera travels through a single 3D world as the user scrolls.

Core Concept

Instead of separate pages or canvases, the entire homepage is one Three.js scene. Scrolling moves the camera through different "zones" of that 3D world — each zone corresponds to a section. Text and UI elements float as HTML overlays on top of the canvas.

SCROLL JOURNEY ↓
Zone 1 — Hero  Operator + giant screen, slow camera orbit
Zone 2 — What We Do  Camera pulls back, control tower revealed
Zone 3 — How It Works  Camera flies into glowing process flow nodes
Zone 4 — Services  Floating 3D panels materialize in space
Zone 5 — Why Us  Stats emerge as floating 3D geometry
Zone 6 — Case Studies  Camera zooms into floating data panels
Zone 7 — Team  Abstract low-poly avatars orbit in space
Zone 8 — Contact CTA  Camera pulls way back, full scene visible
