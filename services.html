WhatsApp AI Agents

Your front desk, billing counter and booking line, all on WhatsApp. Open 24/7.

Your customers already live on WhatsApp. Our AI agents turn that chat into your most efficient team member. They book appointments, collect payments, answer order-status queries and record every transaction automatically. No app to install, no forms, no hold music. They understand English, Hindi, Urdu and Hinglish, including voice notes.

Think of an agent as a coordinator that never sleeps. It takes over the repetitive work of front desks, cashiers and booking counters so your team can focus on real work.

One number. Zero queues. Every conversation recorded. Customers message. The agent handles it. Your books update themselves. A coordinator that works 24/7 at a fraction of a salary.

What our WhatsApp agents do

📅

Bookings & queues

Book, reschedule and cancel appointments in natural language, with automatic capacity management and estimated arrival times.

💳

Payments in chat

Collect payments through GPay/UPI links and QR codes, confirm them instantly and send digital receipts and invoices.

📦

Live order status

Customers ask "where is my order?" and get a live answer from your ERP or sheet.

📒

Automatic bookkeeping

Every booking and payment is logged, with daily and monthly summaries sent to the owner on WhatsApp.

⏰

Reminders & follow-ups

Appointment reminders to cut no-shows, plus feedback requests, recall reminders and offers to past customers.

🛍️

Catalogue & ordering

Customers browse your catalogue and place orders in chat.

🎙️

Voice & languages

Understands voice notes in English, Hindi, Urdu and Hinglish.

🔑

Owner control by chat

Change timings, capacity and holidays over WhatsApp, with confirm-before-change safety.

🤝

Human handoff & dashboard

A live operator dashboard to monitor chats and take over from the AI at any moment.

🔗

Integrations

Sync with Google Sheets, your ERP or your CRM, and route one number across multiple branches or doctors.

📊

Analytics

Peak hours, no-show rate, revenue and repeat customers at a glance.

Works for: clinics, salons, restaurants, gyms, schools, repair shops, distributors and travel businesses.

Solution 02 · Travel

AI Travel Booking Agent

Your travel desk, in a conversation. Every mode, every border, every hour.

An AI agent that books flights, buses and trains, domestic and international, through plain conversation. A traveller says "Mumbai to Dubai next Friday, window seat, under ₹30k" in any language, and the agent searches, compares, books and issues the ticket. It then manages the journey: changes, cancellations, refunds and day-of-travel questions, all handled 24/7 without queues or call-centre waits.

  • Search and compare flights, buses and trains across operators
  • Book in natural language, including multi-city and international trips
  • Handle refunds, cancellations and rescheduling, with fare-rule explanations
  • 24/7 customer service: PNR and booking status, delay alerts, baggage and visa FAQs
  • Take payments and send e-tickets and invoices in chat
  • Multiple languages and voice support
  • Escalate complex cases to a human agent with full context
  • Log every booking and refund for automatic bookkeeping
  • Runs on WhatsApp, web chat or your own app

Solution 03 · Restaurants

AI Restaurant Agent

Your waiter, cashier and order desk, on WhatsApp. Never closed, never busy.

Diners message your restaurant number the way they would talk to a waiter: "Table for 4 tonight at 8", "one butter chicken and two naan, home delivery" or "where's my order?" The agent shows the menu, takes the booking or order, collects payment and keeps the kitchen and your books in sync. Peak-hour phone rush, missed orders and manual order slips go away.

  • Table reservations: book, change and cancel with automatic capacity management
  • Menu and ordering with customisations and combos, for dine-in, takeaway and delivery
  • GPay/UPI payments in chat with instant confirmation and digital bills
  • Live order status: "Preparing", "Out for delivery" and ETAs
  • Orders reach the kitchen or POS in a clean format, with a dashboard for staff takeover
  • Automatic bookkeeping with daily sales summaries sent to the owner
  • Owner control by chat: menu changes, "sold out" items, timings and holidays
  • Booking reminders, review requests and "we miss you" offers
  • Complaints and refunds handled in chat, escalated to a manager when needed
  • Reports on best sellers, peak hours and repeat customers

Built for: restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, bakeries and catering businesses.

How do you integrate AI into our existing business data and systems?

+

We build secure, privacy-first AI layers (such as Retrieval-Augmented Generation and multi-agent orchestrators) that interface with your existing SQL databases, ERPs, or internal documents. Your data remains strictly private and is never used to train public models.

What kind of custom AI solutions do you build?

+

We specialize in enterprise RAG systems, autonomous agentic workflows built with LangGraph, natural language to SQL business intelligence engines, automated WhatsApp/Telegram action bots, and predictive analytics platforms.

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

+

Yes! We work with businesses of all sizes, from newly formed startups looking to build their first app, to local shops and organizations in Ratnagiri needing websites or internal systems.

Can you build custom software for my business?

+

Absolutely. We build customized database systems, inventory trackers, customer portals, and billing systems designed specifically to match how your business operates daily.
