Engineered for Bottom-Line Profit, Not Tech Buzzwords

Traditional enterprise software costs tens of lakhs and takes months of training that field staff and dealers resist. Billing AI connects directly to where your customers and staff already work: everyday messaging apps, backed by industrial-grade accounting logic.

📊

1. Smart Business Planner

Avoid painful capital lock-in. Billing AI dynamically correlates past sales velocity, seasonal trends, and supplier lead times to guide purchase orders before you run out of cash cows.

  • Automated Reorder Alerts: Alerts purchase teams before critical high-margin SKUs go out of stock.
  • Tiered Wholesale Pricing: Handles loose units, half-cartons, full-cartons, and stuffed packing rules effortlessly.
  • Supplier PO Generation: Converts back-orders into supplier purchase orders in one click.
💼

2. Autonomous Accounts Keeper

A full double-entry ledger that operates in real time. Every order placed, packed, or returned updates debtor ledger balances, maintains audit trails, and protects cashflow.

  • Credit Limit Guardrails: Stops risky dispatches when buyer credit limits or overdue balances exceed thresholds.
  • Automated Tax Invoicing: Creates compliant GST tax invoices (PDF) with accurate state-code tax calculations.
  • Payment Reconciliation: Logs bank transfers, cash receipts, and counter entries with instant digital receipts.
⚡

3. Executive Insights Generator

Get clear operational answers without waiting for accountants to compile spreadsheets. Receive an automated executive pulse every morning directly on your phone.

  • 8:00 AM WhatsApp Briefing: Yesterday's sales revenue, cash collected, pending dispatches, and overdue accounts.
  • Dead Stock & Velocity Radar: Flags slow-moving inventory draining warehouse space and tied-up working capital.
  • Buyer Retention Radar: Alerts sales directors when dependable wholesale accounts stop ordering on schedule.
📱

4. Zero-Friction B2B Ordering

No complex app to install from an App Store. Wholesale buyers, retailers, and on-field sales reps access visual product catalogs directly through WhatsApp and Telegram Web MiniApps.

  • Interactive Digital Catalog: High-res photo galleries, SKU variants, live carton stock, and instant search.
  • 100% User Adoption: Works immediately for every retailer without technical onboarding or manuals.
  • Natural Language Ordering: Text or voice an order naturally: "5 cartons Red Gold 077, bill to Ramesh Kolkata".
🚚

5. Multi-Warehouse & Dispatch Automation

Maintain pristine inventory synchronization across central distribution hubs, regional warehouses, and consignment godowns.

  • Real-time Stock Reservation: Prevents overselling when two wholesale buyers order identical high-demand lots.
  • Transport & Logistics Carrier Assignment: Attaches preferred transport agencies, vehicle details, and LR numbers.
  • Packing & Dispatch Slips: Automatically sends pick-and-pack lists to warehouse teams for immediate fulfillment.
🔒

6. Enterprise Security & Architecture

Engineered with strict tenant isolation, bank-grade encryption, and resilient failover. Your commercial data, pricing formulas, and customer ledgers remain entirely private.

  • Isolated Multi-Tenant DB: Dedicated data segregation for each business unit and subsidiary.
  • Seamless ERP Integration: Connects with existing Tally, Busy, SAP, or operates as a standalone cloud ERP.
  • Automated Cloud Backups: Point-in-time disaster recovery with 99.95% operational uptime commitment.
